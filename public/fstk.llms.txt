# Foodies

Foodies is a web app for browsing recipes by category, saving favorites, and publishing your own dishes.

## Pages

- [Home](https://fd-web.fstk.run.place/): hero, categories, and featured recipes
- [Recipes](https://fd-web.fstk.run.place/recipes): recipe catalog with filters

## Notes

- Sign in is required to add a recipe or manage a profile.
- Do not list private URLs such as /recipe/add or /user/:id.
